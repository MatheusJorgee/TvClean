# TCL FHD 2021, product C06_2K_GB, Android TV 11 (AR2101),
# MediaTek MT9221 (mt5867), 943 MB RAM, low_ram. Firmware V8-T221T01.
# Close twin of cleanyourtv examples/tcl-40s5400a (same platform, EU firmware).
#
# Apply:  .\tv\debloat.ps1 -TvHost <ip>
# Undo:   .\tv\debloat.ps1 -TvHost <ip> -Undo
# Everything is DISABLED (pm disable-user), never uninstalled.
#

#

# com.tcl.system.server com.tcl.keyhelp  remote-key plumbing

# com.tcl.settings com.tcl.suspension    picture/sound panel, quick panel
